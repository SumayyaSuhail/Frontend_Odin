Pancake

Description

Pancakes are a universally loved breakfast staple made from a simple starch-based batter cooked on a hot griddle or frying pan.

Whether you love them thick and fluffy, thin and delicate, or are looking to order them nearby, here is a breakdown of recipes and local highlights.

Ingredients

  • All purpose Flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Milk
  • Egg
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Combine wet ingredients and dry ingredients separately.
  2. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
  3. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  4. Scoop batter into the pan.
  5. Flip your pancakes once you see small bubbles forming and popping on top.
  6. Cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown.
